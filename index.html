Privacy Policy for Twinkle Tok

Effective Date: February 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Twinkle Tok collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our application, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

Twinkle Tok is a social platform featuring live streaming, video calls, and a rewards system. We prioritize the security of your personal and financial data.

1. Data Collection & Authentication

To provide a secure experience, we collect the following personal and sensitive information:

  • Account Authentication: We collect your Username, Email Address, and Password to create and verify your unique user identity.
  • Media Access: Real-time access to your Camera and Microphone is required to facilitate live streams and video calls.
  • Financial Data: We track your Wallet Balance and Purchase History (funding and rewards) to prevent fraud and manage in-app points.

2. Data Usage & Sharing

We use your data strictly for app functionality, account management, and community safety. We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with third-party service providers only when necessary to process payments (Google Play Billing) or transmit live media.

3. Security & Encryption

All data collected is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols to protect your information from unauthorized access.

4. Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play’s user data policies, you have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

What happens when you delete:

  • Your Email, Username, and Password are permanently removed from our servers.
  • Your Wallet Balance and Reward History are wiped and cannot be recovered.
  • All saved media or profile content is deleted.

How to initiate deletion:

1. In-App: Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account.

2. Web Request: Send an email from your registered address to the contact below with the subject "Account Deletion Request."
