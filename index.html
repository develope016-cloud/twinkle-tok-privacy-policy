Privacy Policy for Twinkle Tok

Effective Date: February 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Twinkle Tok collects, uses, and protects your information during video calls, live streams, and participation in our rewards program.

By using Twinkle Tok, you consent to the collection of media (audio/video) and account data required to facilitate live services.

1. Sensitive Data Collection

To provide our core features, we collect and process the following sensitive information:

  • Camera & Microphone: Accessed only during live streams and video calls to transmit your audio and video to other participants.
  • Live Content: We may temporarily process live stream data to ensure community safety and policy compliance.
  • Authentication: Email, username, and password are required for account creation and login.

2. Rewards & Points System

When you earn or redeem reward points within Twinkle Tok, we collect:

  • Transaction History: Logs of points earned, spent, or gifted.
  • Financial Info: If you make purchases, data is handled by secure third-party processors (e.g., Google Play Billing). We do not store your full credit card details on our servers.

3. Data Usage & Third Parties

We use your data to facilitate real-time communication and reward distribution. We may share data with:

  • Streaming Service Providers: Technical partners who help transmit live video data.
  • Fraud Prevention: To prevent abuse of the rewards system.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Twinkle Tok is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a minor has created an account, it will be terminated immediately.

5. Mandatory Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play requirements, we provide a clear path for data removal. You can delete your account and all associated data (including points and profile info) directly within the app settings or by contacting us via email.

6. Security

We use modern encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect your live data and login credentials. We follow industry best practices to ensure your video calls remain private.
